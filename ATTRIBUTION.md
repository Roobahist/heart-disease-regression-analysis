# Attribution and dataset provenance

**Original collaborative project (November 2023)**: David Yang,
Arman Jahangiri, Mojtaba Kanani Sarcheshmeh.

Original code source:
https://github.com/davidhy8/heart-regression

`legacy/regression_analysis.Rmd` and `legacy/data_visualization.Rmd` are
the historical team files. They are kept unchanged to preserve group
provenance; the separate `scripts/run_analysis.R` is a reimplementation
designed for reproducible execution.

Dataset source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/johnsmith88/heart-disease-dataset

The dataset is an educational, historically collected, UCI Cleveland-derived
benchmark distributed through Kaggle. It is not a contemporaneous clinical cohort.
The public dataset is commonly listed under CC0, but upstream medical-source
provenance should still be acknowledged.

No claim of exclusive authorship of the original research is made.
